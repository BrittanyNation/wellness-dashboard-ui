Design notes

Designed for a quick glance, not a deep dive

One job per view

Overview answers “how is today?”, Schedule answers “what’s on and who’s teaching?”, and Members answers “who needs a nudge?” Each view was scoped before any styling started.

Warm data, not cold data

Most studio tools look like accounting software. Deep plum gives the product calm authority, coral marks action, and apricot softens celebrations — energy that matches a wellness brand without losing contrast.

Readable under real light

Large stat numbers, labeled bars, and text paired with every status color keep the dashboard usable in a bright studio, on a tablet at the front desk, or on a phone.
